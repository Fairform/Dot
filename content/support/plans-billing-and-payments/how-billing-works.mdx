# How billing works

DOT Mobile plans are billed monthly in advance. Here is everything you need to know about how charging works.

## Your renewal date

Your renewal date is the same calendar date each month as the date you first activated your service. For example, if you activated on 15 March, you are charged on the 15th of every month.

If your renewal date falls on a date that does not exist in a given month (for example, the 31st in February), you are charged on the last day of that month.

## What you are charged

You are charged your full monthly plan fee at the start of each billing cycle. There are no usage-based charges, no excess data charges, and no surprise fees.

Your charges each month are:
- Your monthly plan fee
- Any optional add-ons you have purchased (roaming packs, data top-ups)

## How payment is processed

Your nominated card is charged automatically on your renewal date. Payment is processed by Stripe. You will receive a receipt by email after each successful charge.

If a payment fails, we will notify you and retry within 7 days. After 3 failed attempts, your service may be suspended until payment is resolved.

## Invoices

All invoices are available in your account under **Billing**. Each invoice shows your plan name, billing period, amount charged, and GST breakdown.

## Cancellation and refunds

If you cancel your service, it remains active until the end of your current billing period. We do not offer partial-month refunds. There are no exit fees.

<InlineAlert variant="info" title="No lock-in contracts">
All DOT Mobile plans are month-to-month. You can cancel, upgrade, or downgrade at any time.
</InlineAlert>

## Changing your payment method

Update your card details anytime from your account under **Billing → Update payment method**. We recommend updating before your renewal date if your card is expiring.
