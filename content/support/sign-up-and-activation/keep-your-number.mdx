# How to keep your existing mobile number

You can bring your current mobile number to DOT Mobile during checkout. The process is called number porting.

## How it works

1. During checkout, select **Keep my number**
2. Enter your current mobile number and your current provider name
3. Complete the rest of checkout as normal
4. Your eSIM activates immediately with a temporary number
5. Within 1 to 2 business days, your existing number transfers across automatically
6. Your old service cancels automatically once the port completes

You do not need to contact your current provider first. We handle the port request on your behalf.

## What to expect

- Your new DOT Mobile eSIM is live immediately after activation — you can make calls and use data straight away on your temporary number
- Most port requests complete overnight on business days
- You will receive an SMS confirmation when your number has successfully transferred
- Once the port is complete, your old number is live on DOT Mobile

<InlineAlert variant="info" title="Do not cancel your old plan early">
Keep your current plan active until the port completes. Cancelling it early can cause the port to fail.
</InlineAlert>

## What you need

- Your exact current mobile number (10 digits starting with 04)
- The name of your current provider (e.g. Telstra, Optus, Vodafone)
- To be the account holder on your current plan

## Port timing

Ports initiated before 3:00 PM on a business day usually complete the same evening. Ports initiated on weekends or public holidays begin the next business day.
