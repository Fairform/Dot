# How to sign up for DOT Mobile

Signing up takes under 5 minutes. You do everything online — no stores, no waiting.

## What you need

- An eSIM-compatible device
- A government-issued photo ID (Australian driver licence or passport)
- A Visa, Mastercard, or Amex card
- Your current mobile number if you want to keep it

## Step 1 — Choose your plan

Go to [dotmobile.com.au/plans](/plans) and select the plan that suits you. Not sure which one? Most new customers choose **Core** ($42/month, 50 GB). You can switch plans anytime.

## Step 2 — Enter your details

Fill in your name, email address, date of birth, and residential address. These are required by Australian law for SIM activation.

If you want to keep your current mobile number, select **Keep my number** and enter your current number and provider name.

## Step 3 — Verify your identity

Australian telecommunications law requires us to verify your identity before activating a mobile service. You will be prompted to:

1. Upload a photo of your government-issued ID
2. Take a quick selfie for biometric matching

This is handled by our secure identity verification partner and takes about 60 seconds.

## Step 4 — Pay

Your first month is charged immediately. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Payment is processed securely by Stripe.

## Step 5 — Receive your eSIM

Within 5 minutes of payment, you will receive an email from hello@dotmobile.com.au with your eSIM QR code.

Scan the QR code from your phone settings to activate your service. See [how to install your eSIM](/support/esim-and-devices/how-to-install-your-esim) for device-specific steps.

## Keeping your number

If you selected **Keep my number**, your existing number will transfer to DOT Mobile within 1 to 2 business days. Your current service stays active during this time. See [how number porting works](/support/sign-up-and-activation/keep-your-number) for full details.
