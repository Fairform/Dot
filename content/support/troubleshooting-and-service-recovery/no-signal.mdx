# No signal or service

If your DOT Mobile service is showing no signal, work through these steps in order.

## Step 1 — Check network coverage

Visit [dotmobile.com.au/coverage](/coverage) and confirm that your current location has Telstra wholesale network coverage. Some buildings, basements, and remote areas have limited coverage regardless of carrier.

## Step 2 — Restart your device

Turn your phone fully off and back on. This forces your device to re-register with the network and resolves most temporary signal issues.

## Step 3 — Check eSIM settings

1. Go to **Settings → Mobile Data** (iPhone) or **Settings → Connections → SIM manager** (Android)
2. Confirm that DOT Mobile is listed and enabled
3. If DOT Mobile is listed but not enabled, tap it and enable it
4. Confirm that DOT Mobile is set as your default data line

## Step 4 — Check airplane mode

Make sure airplane mode is off. Even a brief toggle (on then off) can help re-establish a network connection.

## Step 5 — Update carrier settings

On iPhone: Go to **Settings → General → About**. If a carrier settings update is available, a prompt will appear. Install it.

On Android: This happens automatically in the background on most devices.

## Step 6 — Reset network settings

If none of the above steps work, reset your network settings. Note that this will erase saved Wi-Fi passwords.

**iPhone:** Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset Network Settings

**Android:** Settings → General Management → Reset → Reset network settings

<InlineAlert variant="warning" title="Resetting network settings removes your eSIM on some devices">
On some Android devices, resetting network settings may remove your eSIM profile. Check your device manufacturer's documentation before proceeding. If your eSIM is removed, contact us to obtain a new QR code.
</InlineAlert>

## Step 7 — Contact support

If you have completed all steps above and still have no signal, email us at support@dotmobile.com.au with:
- Your device model and operating system version
- Your postcode
- A screenshot of your eSIM settings screen

We will investigate and respond within one business day.
