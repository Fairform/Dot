# My eSIM is not activating

If scanning your QR code is not working, or your eSIM is installed but not connecting, work through these steps.

## Before troubleshooting

Confirm your device is on our [compatible devices list](/support/esim-and-devices/device-compatibility). If your device does not support eSIM, the QR code will not work on it.

## QR code not scanning

**Camera not recognising the QR code:**
- Make sure you are scanning from your **device camera or Settings**, not a third-party QR scanner app
- Hold the camera 20 to 30 cm from the screen displaying the QR code
- Make sure the QR code email is displayed full-size, not zoomed out
- Try in a well-lit environment

**QR code scan succeeds but nothing happens:**
- Ensure you have a stable Wi-Fi connection during activation
- Disable any active VPN on your device and try again
- Restart your device and try scanning again

## Error messages during installation

**"This QR code has already been used"** — eSIM QR codes can only be scanned once. If you see this message, your eSIM has already been installed on a device. If that device is not yours, contact us immediately at support@dotmobile.com.au.

**"SIM not supported"** — Your device is either not eSIM-compatible or is carrier-locked. Check with your previous carrier whether your device is unlocked.

**"Activation server unreachable"** — This is usually a temporary network issue. Wait 5 minutes and try again with a different Wi-Fi network.

## eSIM installed but showing No Service

See our dedicated guide: [eSIM installed but showing no service](/support/troubleshooting-and-service-recovery/esim-showing-no-service).

## QR code is expired or lost

QR codes expire 90 days after issue. If your code has expired or you cannot find the email, see [my QR code has expired or I cannot find it](/support/esim-and-devices/qr-code-expired-or-lost).
