# How to install your eSIM

Installing your DOT Mobile eSIM takes about 3 minutes. You need a Wi-Fi connection and your eSIM QR code email nearby.

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- A compatible device (check our [device compatibility list](/support/esim-and-devices/device-compatibility))
- Your eSIM QR code email from DOT Mobile
- An active Wi-Fi connection

<InlineAlert variant="info" title="Did not receive your email?">
Check your spam folder. If it is not there, you can resend it from your account page or contact us at support@dotmobile.com.au.
</InlineAlert>

## Installing on iPhone

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone
2. Tap **Mobile Data**
3. Tap **Add eSIM** (or **Add Mobile Plan**)
4. Tap **Use QR Code**
5. Scan the QR code from your DOT Mobile activation email
6. Tap **Continue** through the prompts
7. When asked to label your plan, enter **DOT Mobile**
8. Set DOT Mobile as your primary data line when prompted

Your iPhone will connect to the network within 30 seconds.

## Installing on Android (Pixel and others)

1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Network and internet**
3. Tap **SIMs** then **Add SIM**
4. Tap **Download instead**
5. Scan your DOT Mobile QR code
6. Follow the on-screen prompts to activate

## Installing on Samsung Galaxy

1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Connections**
3. Tap **SIM manager**
4. Tap **Add mobile plan**
5. Tap **Scan carrier QR code**
6. Scan your DOT Mobile QR code and follow the prompts

## After installing

Once installed, go to **Settings → Mobile Data** (iPhone) or **Settings → Network → SIMs** (Android) and confirm that DOT Mobile is set as your default data line.

If you see **No Service** after installing, restart your device. This resolves most activation issues.

<InlineAlert variant="warning" title="QR codes can only be scanned once">
Once you scan and activate your eSIM QR code, it cannot be reused. If you need to reinstall, you will need a new QR code from your account.
</InlineAlert>
