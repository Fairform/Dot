# Device compatibility

DOT Mobile is eSIM-only. To use our service your device must support eSIM activation.

## How to check if your device supports eSIM

**On iPhone:** Go to Settings → General → About. Scroll down and look for an EID number. If an EID is present, your iPhone supports eSIM.

**On Android:** Go to Settings → About phone → SIM status (or similar, varies by manufacturer). Look for an EID number. Its presence confirms eSIM support.

## Compatible iPhone models

All iPhone models from iPhone XS (2018) onwards support eSIM, including:

- iPhone 16 series, iPhone 15 series, iPhone 14 series, iPhone 13 series, iPhone 12 series
- iPhone SE (2nd generation and 3rd generation)
- iPhone 11 series, iPhone XS, iPhone XS Max, iPhone XR

iPhone X, iPhone 8, and earlier models do not support eSIM.

## Compatible Android models

**Google Pixel:** Pixel 3 and later (Pixel 3, 3a, 4, 4a, 5, 6, 6a, 7, 7a, 8, 9 series)

**Samsung Galaxy:** Most Galaxy S20 and later, Galaxy Z Fold and Flip series (all generations), Galaxy Note 20 series, Galaxy A54 and A55

**Other brands:** Many recent devices from OnePlus, Sony, Motorola, and Nothing support eSIM. Check your device specifications on the manufacturer's website.

## Carrier locking

Your device must be unlocked (not locked to another carrier) to activate a DOT Mobile eSIM. If you purchased your device outright or have finished your contract with your previous carrier, it is likely already unlocked.

To confirm, contact your previous carrier and ask if your device is unlocked for all networks.

<InlineAlert variant="warning" title="Prepaid devices may be locked">
Devices purchased as part of a prepaid bundle from another carrier are often SIM-locked. Contact your previous carrier before signing up.
</InlineAlert>

## Tablets and iPads

iPads with cellular capability (iPad Pro, iPad Air 4th gen and later, iPad mini 6th gen and later, iPad 9th gen and later) support eSIM for data services. Voice calls and SMS are not available on tablets.
